# Market Validation & GTM

## Objective

Validate that brands will pay for unit-level product identity before building a broad warranty/RMA suite.

## ICP v1

Target 5–100 employee brands, roughly 100–5,000 orders/month, selling durable or counterfeit-sensitive products typically worth $50–$2,000.

Priority verticals:
1. consumer electronics/audio;
2. 3D printers/maker hardware;
3. e-bikes/scooters/mobility;
4. automotive aftermarket;
5. professional tools/equipment;
6. premium beauty devices;
7. fitness equipment;
8. watches/jewelry/premium accessories.

Positive signals:
- own-brand physical products;
- warranty >= 12 months;
- serial/IMEI/lot process already exists or is needed;
- Shopify plus Amazon/retail/distributors;
- product registration form, warranty page or authenticity concerns;
- manual Google Forms/Sheets/email workflow.

## Discovery target

Build a first dataset of **60 qualified brands**:
- 20 electronics/maker;
- 10 mobility;
- 10 auto/tools;
- 10 beauty/fitness;
- 10 premium/counterfeit-sensitive.

Contact 30 first, seek 15 substantive conversations and 5 deeper workflow interviews.

### Interview questions
- How are individual units identified today?
- Where are serials created/stored?
- How do customers activate warranty?
- How are Amazon/retail purchases handled?
- What happens when a proof of purchase is missing?
- Have duplicate/fake serials or counterfeit claims occurred?
- What is manual today?
- What breaks at higher volume?
- Which existing software was tried and why was it rejected?
- What would make you pay $19/$49/$99 monthly?

Do not pitch before understanding the workflow. After pain is confirmed, offer a design-partner pilot.

## Prospect sources

Use public business information from:
- Shopify storefronts and public warranty/registration pages;
- LinkedIn company pages and relevant public employees;
- Amazon brand storefronts;
- Google search;
- industry directories and trade-show exhibitor lists;
- public company contact pages.

Do not collect private personal data or bypass platform restrictions.

## Outreach sequence

Touch 1 — workflow question, not product pitch.  
Touch 2 — short follow-up with one observed signal.  
Touch 3 — 30–60 second mock/demo showing scan -> verify -> register.  
Touch 4 — design-partner invitation.

Primary target roles:
- founder/CEO for small brands;
- Head of Operations;
- Ecommerce Manager;
- Customer Experience / Warranty lead;
- Operations/After-sales lead;
- Product/Hardware Operations.

## Landing smoke test

Primary CTA: **Join the design-partner pilot**.

Value propositions to A/B test:
1. "Give every product a verifiable identity."
2. "Serials, authenticity and warranty in one product registry."
3. "Stop managing product registration in spreadsheets."

Capture:
- work email;
- store/site URL;
- monthly unit volume band;
- current sales channels;
- current serial/warranty workflow;
- willingness to pilot.

## Paid acquisition

Paid ads are validation, not scale.

Initial cap: **$100–$150 total** after organic/message-market testing produces a credible landing conversion.

Test:
- Google Search: high-intent queries around Shopify product registration, warranty registration, serial number tracking and authenticity QR;
- Meta/LinkedIn only for retargeting or extremely tight operator/ecommerce audiences after message validation.

Stop conditions:
- no qualified lead after a meaningful click sample;
- CPC makes projected CAC incompatible with $19–$99 pricing;
- leads are mostly consumers rather than brands.

Do not spend to compensate for weak positioning.

## Success gates

Proceed from discovery to pilot when:
- >=5 qualified brands independently describe materially similar pain;
- >=2 agree to a real pilot/design-partner trial;
- at least one pricing conversation does not reject the target range on principle.

Proceed from pilot to public launch when:
- serial -> verification -> registration journey is used with real units;
- at least 2 pilots reach repeated usage;
- support burden remains compatible with self-service;
- one or more pilots signal willingness to pay or convert.

## Metrics

Acquisition: qualified visits, CTA conversion, qualified lead rate.  
Sales: response rate, interview rate, pilot acceptance, time-to-pilot.  
Activation: first product, first serial batch, first verified scan, first registration.  
Value: units serialized, successful verifications, warranty registrations.  
Retention: active brands and repeated serial batches.  
Economics: ARPU, infra per brand, support time, CAC by channel.
